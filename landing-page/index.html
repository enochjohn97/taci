Who We Are

Building Trust, Delivering Excellence

TACI Petroleum Company Limited is a leading petroleum marketing and freighting enterprise operating across Nigeria since 2001. Based in Kaduna State on the Kaduna-Kachia Road in Kujama, we pride ourselves on reliable, timely fuel delivery to retailers, industries, and corporate clients nationwide.

With a fleet of 30+ trucks and tank capacities ranging from 40,000 to 45,000 litres plus, we ensure safe, efficient transportation of premium petroleum products. Our commitment to quality, safety, and customer satisfaction has made us a trusted partner in Nigeria's energy sector.
